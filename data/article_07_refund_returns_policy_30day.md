# Refund & Returns Policy (Current — 30-Day Policy)
**Slug:** `refund-and-returns-policy-30day-v2`  
**Last Updated:** 2026-08-01  
**Applies To:** All standard subscriptions, SaaS cloud plans, and software licenses created or renewed after August 1, 2024.

---

## 1. Overview & Standard 30-Day Window
DocuMind / Cloudflare customer subscriptions are backed by our **30-Day Satisfaction Guarantee**. Customers on active 2025/2026 commercial terms are entitled to request a full refund within **thirty (30) calendar days** of the initial transaction or recurring billing renewal date.

### Covered Plans:
- **Cloudflare Pro Monthly / Annual** ($49/mo, $588/yr)
- **Cloudflare Business Add-ons** ($200/mo)
- **Digital Software Licenses & API Tokens**
- **Professional Services Onboarding** (if unused hours remain)

---

## 2. Refund Eligibility Criteria
To qualify for an automated Tier-1 refund:
1. The request must be submitted within **30 days** of the billing invoice date (`days_since_purchase <= 30`).
2. The account must not have active abuse or terms of service violations.
3. The invoice status must be `COMPLETED` and not already `REFUNDED` or under formal financial dispute.

---

## 3. Exclusions & Limitations
- **Hardware & Security Keys:** Physical Titan keys and hardware tokens are subject to a **14-day inspection window** and must be returned unopened in original packaging.
- **Transactions Beyond 30 Days:** If `days_since_purchase > 30`, automated Tier-1 refunds are strictly **ineligible**. The assistant must decline the cash refund and offer account credit or escalate to a billing supervisor.
- **Chargeback Disputes:** Any transaction with an active bank dispute (`DISPUTED`) must be immediately escalated to Tier-2 Billing Operations. Do NOT promise a refund.
