# Refund & Returns Policy (Retired / Grandfathered — 14-Day Terms)
**Slug:** `refund-and-returns-policy-14day-legacy`  
**Last Updated:** 2024-01-15 (SUPERSEDED for modern accounts; ACTIVE strictly for legacy pre-August 2024 accounts and physical hardware)  
**Applies To:** Grandfathered corporate contracts executed prior to August 1, 2024 that have not signed Master Services Agreement v2.0, and all physical hardware shipments.

---

## 1. Legacy 14-Day Window Terms
Under our historical 2023/2024 commercial policy, customer refund eligibility was capped at **fourteen (14) calendar days** from the invoice date. 

> **CRITICAL COMPLIANCE NOTICE FOR AGENTS & DEFLECTION ASSISTANTS:**
> This article remains active in the public knowledge base solely because legacy grandfathered accounts remain bound by these terms until contract renewal.
> **DO NOT** cite this article or apply the 14-day restriction to accounts on modern terms (`terms_version: "30-day-current"`). Quoting this retired window to modern customers is a major compliance failure that leads directly to executive escalation.

---

## 2. Legacy Account Eligibility Rules
- **Contract Signature Date:** Before August 1, 2024.
- **Refund Window:** Strict **14 calendar days** (`days_since_purchase <= 14`).
- **Requests between 15 and 30 days:** Denied under legacy terms, unless account executes a migration addendum to the current 30-day terms.

---

## 3. Physical Hardware Devices (All Accounts)
- Physical security keys (YubiKey, Titan keys), hardware 2FA dongles, and server appliances remain subject to the **14-day delivery return window** regardless of subscription terms.
- Returned hardware must include original seals and serial numbers.
