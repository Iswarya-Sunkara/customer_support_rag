## 2. Entitlement & Shipping Claims Precondition
Before providing estimated arrival dates or processing replacement shipments, Tier-1 agents and automated assistants must verify order status in billing tools.
- Never promise shipping speed upgrades without checking customer tier (`get_customer_tier`).
- Priority accounts receive complimentary Express Air on all hardware replacements.
