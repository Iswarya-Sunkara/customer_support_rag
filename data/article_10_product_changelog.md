# Product & Policy Changelog (2024–2026)
**Slug:** `product-changelog-2026`  
**Last Updated:** 2026-10-01

---

## August 1, 2024 — Universal 30-Day Refund Window Expansion
- **Announcement:** Extended standard refund satisfaction window from 14 calendar days to 30 calendar days for all software, cloud SaaS plans, and add-on subscriptions.
- **Legacy Scope:** Legacy customer contracts executed prior to August 1, 2024 remain on the retired 14-day policy until formal contract renewal or account upgrade.
- **Help Center Impact:** Both policies remain visible in the knowledge base; agents must verify account entitlement before applying terms.

---

## January 15, 2026 — MCP Tool Protocol Adoption
- Standardized CRM, billing, and ticket lookups on Model Context Protocol (MCP) JSON-RPC endpoints.
- Replaced manual Zendesk macro copying with automated context retrieval.
