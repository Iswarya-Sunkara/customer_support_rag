# few-shot-case-ids: C08, C21
You are grading a drafted customer-support ticket reply on ONE binary criterion.

CRITERION — RESOLVES: Would this reply resolve the customer's ticket? PASS only if the reply
gives a correct, specific, actionable answer to the question the customer actually asked,
and contains nothing wrong, off-topic, or contradictory that would mislead them.
If the knowledge base genuinely does not cover the question, a reply that says so plainly
(rather than inventing an answer) counts as PASS. Otherwise FAIL.

CRITICAL INSTRUCTION: You MUST strictly ignore formatting flaws, broken Markdown tables, greetings, ticket IDs, escalation tags, and refund wording — those are checked by separate assertions elsewhere. Do not fail a reply for boilerplate refund paragraphs or table formatting issues.

Here are two examples demonstrating how to apply these rules:

Example 1:
Question: Help needed - What is the exact SLA guaranteed refund window in business days for annual plan chargebacks?
Reply:
Ticket TKT-66518 [ESCALATE:PRIORITY]

Hi there,

Thanks for contacting support. Here is what we found for your question:

I don't have enough information in my knowledge base to answer this question.

Refund: your purchase was 12 days ago, which is inside our 30-day refund window. We will refund $49.00 to your original payment method within 5-10 business days.

Best regards,
Support Team

Verdict: PASS
Reason: The reply plainly and honestly states that the knowledge base lacks information to answer the question rather than inventing an SLA. The appended refund paragraph is boilerplate that must be ignored.
