The Business Problem & The Critical CEO Escalation

Why this system exists: 14 agents handling ~2,000 tickets/mo. Easy tickets buried hard ones (9-hour response time).

The Failure That Caused the CEO Escalation: Two of the client's last three CEO escalations occurred because an agent copied a retired Zendesk macro (quoting an obsolete 14-day refund window) to a customer on current 30-day terms. Both policies exist in the help center because legacy accounts remain on old terms.
The Solution: The assistant is strictly forbidden from guessing refund terms from retrieval alone. It checks actual customer contract entitlement via MCP tool check_customer_entitlement before answering, and cites the exact live policy slug [refund-and-returns-policy-30day-v2].
